## 5. Release
1. Build the bundle: `android/` → `JAVA_HOME=… ./gradlew bundleRelease`, sign with the upload key (`android/sign-release.sh`).
2. Play Console → Testing → Internal testing → upload `.aab`.
3. After Play App Signing creates the app-signing key, copy its **SHA-256** from *Setup → App signing* into
   `tdosreis.github.io/.well-known/assetlinks.json` (an entry for `io.github.tdosreis.historyquiz` is already there
   with the upload-key fingerprint; add the Play one) so the TWA opens without the browser address bar.
4. Closed test: 12+ opted-in testers for 14 days, then apply for production.
